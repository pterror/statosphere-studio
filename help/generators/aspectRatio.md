# Aspect Ratio

The desired aspect ratio for the generated image. Valid values: `21:9`, `16:9`, `3:2`, `5:4`, `1:1`, `4:5`, `2:3`, `9:16`, `9:21`.

- `16:9` — widescreen, good for scene backgrounds
- `1:1` — square, good for profile portraits
- `9:16` — vertical, good for tall character art
